## 2. Neuro-Design & Color Psychology
*Why Minito looks the way it does.*

Our UI is calibrated to lower cortisol (stress) and spike dopamine (reward).

| Element | Color / Hex | Neurological Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Void (BG)** | `#121212` (Charcoal) | **Sensory Deprivation.** Unlike pure black, it reduces eye strain and visual noise, allowing focus. |
| **The Spark (Action)** | `#8B5CF6` (Neon Purple) | **Curiosity.** Triggers the "Novelty Seeking" center of the ADHD brain. |
| **The Reward (Done)** | `#34D399` (Mint Green) | **Safe Dopamine.** Signals safety and completion without the aggression of standard green. |
| **The Correction** | `#F59E0B` (Amber) | **Forgiveness.** Used for errors/internet loss. Never use Red (Failure). |

## 3. The Psychological "Hooks"
*How we get them addicted (in a healthy way).*

### 3.1 The "Minitize" Moment (Activation)
* **Old Way:** "Create a Task." (Boring, feels like work).
* **Minito Way:** **"Minitize It!"** (Magic, feels like a hack).
* **Rule:** The user must feel like they are "cheating" the difficulty of life.

### 3.2 Behavioral Momentum (The Start)
* **The Frictionless Entry:** The AI ensures Step 1 is always "Stupid Easy" (e.g., "Stand up").
* **Mechanism:** Crossing the threshold of Step 1 releases the activation energy needed for Step 2.

### 3.3 Variable Reward System (The Hook)
* **Predictable:** Confetti on every task. (Boring after a while).
* **Variable:** Random "Success Messages" from our pool ("You Own Your Brain", "Monster Slain").
* **Result:** The brain stays engaged because it's curious: *"What will it say this time?"*
