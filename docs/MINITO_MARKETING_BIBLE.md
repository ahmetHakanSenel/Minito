## 🧠 1. Strategic Framework (The "Anti-Productivity" Philosophy)
*Why Minito? Why Now?*

People don't need another to-do list; they need a **"Starting Mechanism."**
The market is full of apps that manage *tasks*. Minito manages the *brain*.

### The Minito Trinity (Value Prop)
1.  **Capture (The Net):** "Don't let it float in your head." Capture the anxiety in 10 seconds.
2.  **Transform (Minitize):** "Don't list it, break it." Turn a monster task into 3-9 atomic, dopamine-fueled steps.
3.  **Sustain (The Flow):** "Don't break the chain." Simple daily rituals that reward momentum, not perfection.

## ⚡ 4. Product-Led Growth (The First 60 Seconds)
*The app must sell itself before the user pays a cent.*

### The "Aha!" Moment Flow
1.  **Input:** User types "Clean Room" (The Void).
2.  **Magic:** User clicks "Minitize It". AI generates "1. Stand up".
3.  **Victory:** User clicks "DONE" on Step 1. -> **Confetti Explosion.**
4.  **Result:** User is hooked. "I just did something."

### Neuro-Design Principles
* **Low Stimulus:** Dark Mode (`#121212`) reduces sensory overload.
* **High Direction:** Only *one* step visible at a time (Tunnel Vision).
* **Forgiving UI:** Error states use **Amber/Orange** (Correction), never Red (Failure).

## 📢 5. Distribution Engine (Marketing Channels)

### A. The Content Motor (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)
*Dominate vertical video with "Relatable Pain".*

* **Format 1: "POV: ADHD Paralysis"**
    * *Visual:* Someone staring at a messy desk for hours.
    * *Cut:* Opens Minito -> Types "Clean Desk" -> Clicks Minitize.
    * *Result:* Action starts.
* **Format 2: "Body Doubling" (Viral Potential)**
    * *Visual:* No talking. Just a screen recording of Minito + You working.
    * *Caption:* "Let's Minitize this task together. 3... 2... 1..."
* **Format 3: "The Visualizer"**
    * *Visual:* Comparison of "A long scary list" vs. "Minito's Step Cards".

### B. App Store Optimization (ASO)
* **Keywords:** ADHD Planner, Focus Timer, Executive Dysfunction, Dopamine.
* **Screenshots:** Use Neon highlights. Show the *confetti*, not just the list.
* **Subtitle:** "Don't stress. Just Minitize It."

### C. Referral Loops (Viral Growth)
* **Offer:** "Invite a friend, you BOTH get 1 week of 'Unlimited Minitizing'."
* **Logic:** Two-sided rewards create social obligation.

## 🔄 6. Retention Strategy (The Sticky Factor)

### The "Zeigarnik" Notification
* *Trigger:* User leaves a task half-finished.
* *Message:* "Hey, you left 'Step 3' hanging. It's lonely. Click to finish." (Curiosity > Guilt).

### The Rituals
* **Morning (2 min):** "What is the ONE thing we Minitize today?"
* **Evening (2 min):** "You conquered [X] steps. See you tomorrow, Boss."

## 💰 7. Financial & Metrics (The Scoreboard)

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
1.  **Activation Rate:** % of users who complete *at least one* "Minitize" cycle in Session 1.
2.  **Day-7 Retention:** Do they come back after the novelty wears off?
3.  **K-Factor:** (Invites sent) / (Invites accepted). Target > 1.1.

### Monetization (The Hybrid Model)
* **Free:** 3 Minitizes/Day + Ads (Home/Settings only).
* **Pro:** Unlimited + "Zen Mode" (No Ads) + Future Calendar Sync.
